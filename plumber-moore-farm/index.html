Moore Farm is a newer community along the Waxhaw-Indian Trail Road corridor in western Indian Trail. A real estate listing site describes a mix of single-family homes and townhomes, with most homes dating from 2019 to 2026. If a pipe, drain or fixture in your Moore Farm home needs attention, call (704) 951-1635 and describe the problem.

A home this new should have an easy main shutoff, so find yours today. If water is running, close it and then call.

Where Moore Farm is and which phase you are in

The Town of Indian Trail's project page for Moore Farm Phases III and IV places it near 3311 Waxhaw Indian Trail Road. Phase III is listed at 134 single-family homes and 186 townhomes, and Phase IV at 156 single-family homes, for 476 units in total. Town Council approved the project on October 12, 2021, and the page lists it as under construction.

The same page says Phases I and II were built in Union County, before the Town was involved. The mailing address is still Indian Trail 28079, but your phase decides whether the Town or the county handles permits and street questions. The Town's Planning Department at 704-821-5401 can confirm any address.

What newer homes change about plumbing

Homes finished in the 2020s are generally plumbed to current code with modern fixtures. That does not mean nothing ever goes wrong, and new homes bring their own early questions, starting with who to call.

Builder warranty

Warranty terms vary, so check your closing documents before paying for a repair that the builder may cover. A plumber can still stop active water and document the cause.

Fittings and fixtures

Slow drips at supply connections or a loose fixture can show up in any home, including a new one, and are worth reporting early.

Hot water capacity

Open layouts, lofts and extra baths can push a single heater harder than expected.

Townhomes

Know your own shutoff and tell a neighbor quickly if water may cross a shared wall.

Before you call about a leak

Stop the water

Shut the main valve and drain the lines from the lowest tap.

Warranty contact

Locate the builder's warranty number and your closing date.

Tank trouble

Disconnect power or gas and close the cold-water valve on the heater.

Phase and street

Photograph the leak and note which phase and street you live on.

Moore Farm plumbing questions

Should I call my builder or a plumber first?

If the home is under warranty, contact the builder first for anything that may be covered. If water is actively flowing, close the main valve and call a plumber right away, then tell the builder.

Is my Moore Farm address inside the Town of Indian Trail?

Phases III and IV are in the Town according to its project page. Phases I and II were built in Union County. Call the Town's Planning Department at 704-821-5401 to confirm an address.

Does the HOA handle plumbing inside my home?

Common areas such as the clubhouse and pool belong to the association. Interior plumbing is normally yours, and your covenants and warranty papers mark the boundary.

My hot water runs out when the whole family showers. Why?

The cause can be heater size, a failed element or sediment. A plumber can test the heater and tell you whether repair or a larger unit makes sense. See the water heater installation page.

What should I write down before I call?

Your street and phase, when the problem started, whether the water is off and any photos. That saves time on the call.
