Brandon Oaks sits on the east side of Indian Trail, near Indian Trail-Fairview Road. Livability describes it as 1,346 single-family homes spread across 16 sections, which makes it one of the larger planned communities in town. If a pipe, drain or water heater in your Brandon Oaks home needs attention, call (704) 951-1635 and describe the problem.

If water is running right now, shut off the main valve before anything else. Most homes have it by the meter or at the spot where the supply line comes through the wall. After that, open the lowest faucet to drain the lines and ask on the call whether service is available on your street.

How Brandon Oaks is laid out

The main internal road is Brandon Oaks Parkway. The community is divided into sections with names you will recognize from your deed or HOA mail: Braxton, Carlisle, Summerston I and II, Courtfield, Ashgrove, Fenwick, Morning Creek, Rosewater, Fountainbrook, Bridleside, Cooper Ridge, Currier, Spanish Moss, Forest View and The Gardens.

The Gardens is a smaller section of 63 patio homes, and some of those properties have a second association that handles landscaping in unfenced areas. No published boundary map turned up in our research, so Brandon Oaks is described here by its parkway and section names without exact edges.

What the homes tell you about plumbing

Livability reports that Brandon Oaks homes were built primarily between 1994 and 2014, across ranch, one-and-a-half-story and two-story layouts. One real estate directory lists homes from 1994 through 2023. Sizes run from roughly 1,279 square feet to more than 4,600, with three to seven bedrooms.

That spread matters for one practical reason. A community built over twenty years has water heaters, faucets and supply shutoffs of many different ages, so the right question is how old your own equipment is. Nothing about the neighborhood means your pipes are in poor shape.

Water heater age

Tank water heaters commonly last about 8 to 12 years. The data plate on the tank shows the build date, and an older tank is worth planning around before it leaks.

Slow or gurgling drains

One slow sink is usually local to that fixture. Several slow fixtures at once point toward the main line.

Fixtures and shutoffs

Dripping faucets, running toilets and stiff shutoff valves are common service calls in homes of any age.

Larger homes

More bathrooms mean more fixtures, more supply lines and a bigger hot water demand on one heater.

Water service in Brandon Oaks

According to the Town of Indian Trail, Union County Water supplies most of town, with one named exception elsewhere in town. Brandon Oaks is not listed as that exception, but the name on your water bill is the reliable way to confirm your provider.

Your provider is who to call about a leak at the meter or a water quality question. Pipe beyond the meter is generally the homeowner's responsibility.

Before you call about a leak or backup

Water is flowing

Shut the main valve, then open the lowest tap so the lines drain.

Tank leaking

Cut power or gas to the heater and close the cold valve on top of the tank.

Drain backing up

Stop running water everywhere and keep kids and pets off the wet floor.

Section and street

Note your Brandon Oaks section and street name so the call goes faster.

Brandon Oaks plumbing questions

Does the Brandon Oaks HOA fix plumbing inside my house?

Associations usually look after shared amenities such as the pools and clubhouse, not the pipes in your walls. Your covenants spell out the line, so read them if you are unsure. The extra association in The Gardens deals with landscaping and does not change interior plumbing.

Which sections does this page cover?

Every section of Brandon Oaks, from Braxton and Carlisle through The Gardens. If you are not sure whether your street counts as Brandon Oaks, describe it on the call and ask whether your street is covered.

Which utility supplies water in Brandon Oaks?

The Town of Indian Trail lists Union County Water as the provider for most of the Town. Your water bill shows your own provider and the number to call for meter or main line problems.

My water heater is the original one from the 1990s or 2000s. Should I replace it early?

A tank that is 10 years old or more is worth planning around. If it leaks at the base, replace it right away. See the water heater replacement page for what the work involves.

Who do I call when several drains back up at once?

Stop using water and call. Several fixtures backing up together usually points to the main line, and the sewer line cleaning page explains how that is checked.
