A water heater that is leaking from the tank body cannot be repaired, and the damage grows by the hour. This page is for the moment you find water on the floor, a rusted seam or a heater that has finally given up. Close the cold water valve on top of the tank, switch off the breaker or set the gas control to off, and open a hot tap somewhere in the house to relieve pressure. Then call. If the leak is heavy, shut off the whole house at the main valve and keep water away from any outlets.

When a failed water heater is an emergency

A tank that leaks from the body, the bottom or a seam is finished. So is one that repeatedly trips the relief valve, runs rusty hot water at every tap and sits in a rusted pan. Age matters too. Manufacturers commonly rate a standard tank for roughly 8 to 12 years, and plenty of Indian Trail homes built in the town's fast growth years since 1990 are now carrying their original or second tank.

If you are not sure which side of the line you are on, the water heater repair page explains which faults are worth fixing.

Sizing, fuel and the choice that actually matters

Tank size

A household of one or two often does well with 40 gallons. A family of four with a soaking tub usually needs 50 or more. We size by how many people shower back to back, not by what the old label said.

Gas or electric

We replace like with like unless you want to change. A fuel change moves the job from swap to project, with gas line, venting and electrical work.

Recovery rate

A bigger tank that heats slowly can feel worse than a smaller, faster one. Gas units recover faster than standard electric models.

Venting and space

A gas tank needs correct vent sizing, draft and clearance. A garage unit needs the burner raised or protected according to the manufacturer.

What replacement day looks like

1

Shut off and drain

We stop the water and power or gas, then drain the tank and check the surrounding floor and pan for damage.

2

Remove and haul away

The old tank comes out and leaves with us. Plan for the heavy lift to take two people.

3

Set and connect

The new tank goes on a new pan with new flexible connectors, a new relief valve and a discharge line routed safely.

4

Test and explain

We fill, purge air, check for leaks and combustion on gas, set the temperature and show you the shutoff valves.

Most replacements finish in a single visit when the swap is like for like. Moving the heater, changing fuel or reworking the vent takes longer, and we say so before starting.

The permit question in Union County

Union County Building Code Enforcement states that water heater replacement is exempt from a permit only when there is no change in fuel, energy source, location, capacity or the routing of venting and piping. A straight swap of a 40-gallon gas tank for another 40-gallon gas tank in the same spot fits that description. Upsizing, relocating the unit or moving from electric to gas does not, so call the county or ask us how it applies to your address.

Leaks that mean replace now

Water from the tank body, a wet insulation layer that you can feel through the jacket or a puddle that returns after you wipe it up means the tank has failed. There is no repair for a corroded steel tank. Shut off the cold supply and the power or gas and call. Do not run a leaking tank, since a failing tank can release a lot of water quickly.

If the unit is in an attic, a closet over living space or a finished area, the stakes are higher. A pan with a drain line and a water leak alarm are inexpensive protection on the new tank. We discuss these when we plan the replacement, so the new unit is protected from the first day.

Questions about replacing a water heater here

Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Indian Trail?

Yes. Call the 24/7 line, tell us what you see and we will help you stop the water first. Replacement the same day depends on parts and crew availability, and we tell you honestly what is possible.

Will you remove my old water heater?

Yes. Removal and disposal of the old tank is part of the replacement we quote.

Do I need a permit to replace my water heater?

Union County says a like-for-like swap with no change in fuel, energy source, location, capacity or vent and pipe routing is exempt. Any change in those items likely needs a permit, and we confirm with the county before we start.

Should I switch to tankless?

It depends on your gas supply, venting and daily hot water use. The tankless water heater page lays out the tradeoffs without pushing you either way.

Does the water in Indian Trail shorten tank life?

Union County Water reports very soft water, around 23 ppm hardness, so heavy scale is not the typical problem. Age, anode rod condition and sediment still matter.
