What a Monroe address tells a plumber

Monroe's population was 34,562 in the 2020 census, and the city includes the Monroe Downtown Historic District and the Monroe Residential Historic District, both listed on the National Register of Historic Places. Houses in or near those districts are often decades older than the subdivisions that fill most of Union County. Older homes can carry older supply lines and older drains, and they are worth a camera look when a clog keeps returning.

Newer neighborhoods on the edges of the city look like Indian Trail's, with PVC drains and modern supply lines. The practical difference is that an old house deserves a conversation about pipe material, and a new one deserves a look at fittings and workmanship. We ask what year the house was built when you call, since it shapes what we bring.

What the City of Monroe reports about your water

Most Monroe water comes from the Monroe Lakes, which are Lake Twitty, Lake Lee and Lake Monroe, with an intermittent supply from the Catawba River. The city's FY2023 water quality report says chlorine and ammonia are added to disinfect the water, which means chloramine, and that pH is adjusted to reduce corrosion. It reported a pH of 7.5.

The same report lists lead and copper results from 30 sites in 2023 with none above the action level. It does not list a hardness figure, so we do not quote one. If you want to know the hardness at your tap, ask the Monroe Water Resources office or use a simple test kit.

Emergency prep for Monroe homes

Use these steps while you wait for a plumber. Keep them short and do them in order.

Water is flowing

Close the main valve, usually near the meter or where the line enters the house, and open the lowest faucet to drain the pipes.

Near electrical

Do not stand in water near outlets or the panel. Shut off power at the breaker only if you can reach it dry.

Water heater

Cut power at the breaker or turn the gas control to off, and close the cold supply valve above the tank so it stops refilling.

Sewer backup

Stop using every fixture and keep children and pets out of the room.

Document

Photograph the damage before cleanup and call your insurer after the water is stopped.

Seasonal and neighborhood patterns around Monroe

Cold snaps hit exposed lines in garages, crawlspaces and outside walls, and thaws reveal the splits. Summer thunderstorms test sump pumps and sewer laterals. Along US Highway 74 and US Highway 601, the commercial corridors have restaurants and shops that call for after-hours drain and heater help, which our commercial plumbing service covers.

We also serve the smaller communities between Indian Trail and Monroe, including Stallings, Wesley Chapel, Unionville and Hemby Bridge. If your address is in one of them, call the same line and say where you are.

Monroe plumbing questions

Is emergency service available in Monroe?

Yes. The 24/7 line is answered every day, including nights, weekends and holidays. Tell us your street and what failed, and we will tell you what to shut off while we arrange a plumber.

Do you cover every ZIP code in Monroe?

We serve Monroe's ZIP codes 28110, 28111 and 28112, from the downtown streets near the courthouse out to the neighborhoods along US Highway 74. If your address has a different ZIP, call and we will confirm.

How far is Monroe from your Indian Trail base?

Monroe is southeast of Indian Trail, a short drive along US Highway 74. Travel time depends on traffic and where you are in the city, so the dispatcher gives you a realistic time when you call.

My Monroe home is old. Do I need to worry about the pipes?

Not automatically. Age is a reason to look, not a reason to repipe. A camera look and a pressure check show the real condition.

Does the City of Monroe or my plumber handle a sewer backup?

The city handles the public main. The line on your property is generally yours. If neighbors have the same backup, call the city too.

Where do I find the City of Monroe water quality report?

The city posts its annual report on its website, monroenc.org, and lists the water treatment plant contact in the report.
