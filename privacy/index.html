This policy explains how Indian Trail 24/7 Plumbing handles information on this website and on calls to our line. We keep it short because we collect little.

What this website collects

This website has no forms, accounts or comment boxes. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site. Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited for security and performance.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address and a description of the problem so a plumber can help you. We use that information to respond to your request and to follow up on the work.

Cookies and third parties

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map. Those providers may set cookies or receive technical data under their own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You may ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it. Call (704) 951-1635 or write to contact@247plumberindiantrail.com.

Changes

We may update this page. The date of the latest change is the date the page was last published.
