When something fails in your plumbing, you want to find the right page fast. This index groups our services by problem. Water heater trouble, clogged or backed-up drains, leaks and burst pipes, fixtures and specialty work each have their own page with symptoms, repair methods, local notes and answers to common questions. Our line is open 24/7 for emergencies.

If you are not sure which service you need, call. A plumber can usually tell from a short description which page, and which repair, fits your situation.

How to choose the right service

Start with what you see. No hot water points to the water heater pages. A slow or backing-up drain points to drain and sewer pages. A wet spot, a high bill or a hissing sound points to the leak pages. Planned projects such as a new bathroom or a tankless upgrade point to the installation pages.

Water is flowing somewhere it should not

Shut off the main and read burst pipe repair, then call the 24/7 line.

No hot water or a leaking tank

See water heater repair and water heater replacement.

Drains slow or sewage backing up

See drain cleaning and sewer line cleaning.

A bill that jumped

See leak detection to find water you cannot see.

Serving Indian Trail and nearby towns

We work in Indian Trail and the surrounding part of Union County. Our Monroe page explains how we cover the county seat, and the service areas page lists the other places we reach.

What each group covers

Water heaters. Repair, replacement, planned installation and tankless service. Start with repair when a tank still holds water and replacement when it leaks.

Drains and sewer. Everyday clogs, hydro jetting, main line cleaning, camera inspection and sewer repair. The drain cleaning page is the best place to start when a fixture is slow.

Leaks and pipes. Hidden leak detection, burst pipe repair, water service line repair and repiping. If you are not sure whether you have a leak, read leak detection and do the meter test.

Fixtures. Toilets, faucets, garbage disposals and new fixture installation. These are the quickest repairs and often finish in one visit.

Specialty. Commercial plumbing, sump pump repair and basement waterproofing, which cover businesses and homes with water entry problems.

What is the same on every page

Every service page explains the signs, how a plumber diagnoses the problem, what choices you have and the factors that set the price. Each page also carries local notes drawn from public sources, such as Union County Water's water quality report and the Union County Building Code Enforcement permit page. We list sources by type and never state a rule we cannot back up.

Emergency pages open with what to do while you wait. They say plainly when a problem can wait for a daytime visit and when it cannot. If you are in doubt, call, since a short conversation with a plumber is the quickest way to know.

How pricing works

You get a clear price before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead. The price depends on what failed, how hard it is to reach, the parts needed and the hour of the call. Each service page lists the factors that shape its price, so you know what to expect before you call. We do not give a number without knowing the details, because a number given blind is usually wrong.

Not sure where to start

Call the 24/7 line and describe what you see. A plumber will tell you which page matches your situation, what to shut off and what to do while you wait. If it is not urgent, say so and we will schedule a daytime visit.

Questions people ask before they call

Most callers want to know three things. Is this an emergency, what can I do right now and roughly what will it involve. The pages above answer those in order. Each one opens with the situation, gives the safe first steps and then explains the repair and the factors that move the price.

If you are comparing plumbers, check the license first. North Carolina's State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors keeps a public license search at nclicensing.org, and its office can be reached at 919-875-3612. Then ask what the price includes, who handles any permit and what happens if the plumber finds something else once the wall is open.

Sources behind these pages

According to Union County Building Code Enforcement, permits apply to the addition or change in design of plumbing. Work is built to the State Plumbing Code, which adopts the International Plumbing Code (IPC).

Permits and rules in Union County

Union County Building Code Enforcement says a permit is needed for the addition or change in design of plumbing. It also says a water heater replacement is exempt only when there is no change in fuel, energy source, location, capacity or the routing of venting and piping. Many repairs are exempt, and many installations are not. We tell you which applies to your job before we start and help you with the steps.
