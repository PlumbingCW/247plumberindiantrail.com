Should I Repipe My House? An Indian Trail, NC Guide

A repipe is worth it when the pipes are failing, not when one fitting leaked once.

Repipe decisionPipe materialsHomeowner guide
Talk to a plumber
(704) 951-1635
☎ Call Now

Written by the Indian Trail 24/7 Plumbing team

You should consider repiping your house when leaks keep coming back, when the pipe is a known problem material such as polybutylene or old galvanized steel, when water is rusty or the pressure has dropped, or when you are already opening walls for a remodel. One leak, by itself, is a repair.

If a pipe has burst right now, shut off the main and call. Our burst pipe repair page covers the emergency steps and our repiping page covers the project.

Questions that decide it

How many leaks in a year

Several pinholes in different places mean the system is wearing out.

What material is it

Polybutylene and galvanized steel have poor records. Copper and PEX usually last.

What does the water look like

Brown or orange water from taps points to corroding steel.

How is the pressure

Low pressure throughout can mean internal buildup.

What else is happening

A remodel is the cheapest time to replace pipe.

Pipe materials in homes around here

Houses built in the early and mid 1990s can contain polybutylene, a gray flexible plastic used widely until the mid-1990s and known for failing at fittings. Many homes from the 2000s have CPVC or copper, and newer ones use PEX. Older homes elsewhere may have galvanized steel.

Open an attic access, a crawlspace or a utility closet and look at the pipe near the water heater. A plumber can identify the material and its condition in minutes.

Repair or repipe

If one run is bad and the rest is sound, a partial repipe or a repair can save money. If the same material is failing in several places, replacing it all prevents a string of repairs and water damage. A plumber who is not paid by the foot will tell you honestly which fits your house.

Local note: Union County rules and water

Permits and water chemistry in Indian Trail

Union County Building Code Enforcement states that a permit is needed for the addition or change in design of plumbing. A repipe is normally permitted and inspected. Union County Water's 2023 report listed a near-neutral pH of 7.11 and very soft water at 23 ppm hardness, so aggressive water is not a typical local cause of pipe failure.

That points to fittings, pipe defects and workmanship as the usual causes when a repipe is considered. Indian Trail grew from 1,942 residents in 1990 to 39,997 in the 2020 census, so many homes sit in the age range where first-generation plastic fittings are showing wear.

For a home near the county seat, our Monroe plumber page describes our coverage, and the service areas page lists other places we reach.

What a repipe costs and what changes it

Cost depends on house size, number of fixtures, pipe material and access. Open crawlspaces and attics are easier than finished ceilings and slab homes. Drywall repair is separate from plumbing. A written quote that lists each part avoids surprises.

How to find out what is in your walls

You can learn a lot without opening anything. Check the pipes where they are exposed, near the water heater, in the crawlspace or in the attic. Gray flexible plastic with stamped markings suggests polybutylene. Silver-gray threaded steel suggests galvanized. Reddish-brown metal suggests copper. White or cream rigid plastic suggests CPVC. Colored flexible tubing, usually red, blue or white, suggests PEX.

Photograph what you see and send it to a plumber. A short phone conversation is often enough to say whether a closer look is justified. If you plan to buy a home, ask the seller what material was used and whether any repairs were made.

The costs people overlook

A repipe quote should list the plumbing work, the permit and inspection, the wall and ceiling openings and who patches them. Drywall repair and paint are often separate from the plumbing price. Moving furniture, protecting floors and the days without water all affect how it feels to live through the job.

Alternatives to a full repipe

A partial repipe replaces the worst run and leaves sound sections alone. Replacing just the supply lines to a bathroom while you remodel is a common example. A pressure regulator can reduce stress on joints if high pressure is part of the problem. These options cost less, but they do not solve the issue if the whole system is failing.

Whatever you choose, get the diagnosis in writing and ask the plumber to show you the failed section. A good plumber is happy to explain what they found. Our burst pipe repair page covers what to do if a line fails in the meantime.

Sources behind this guide

Repiping is permitted work under the State Plumbing Code, which adopts the International Plumbing Code (IPC). The permit rule comes from Union County Building Code Enforcement, and the water facts come from the Union County Water 2023 report.

Repiping questions

Is polybutylene pipe a reason to repipe?

It has a record of failure, so many owners replace it. A plumber can confirm what you have.

How long does a repipe take?

A typical house takes a few days. A plumber gives a time estimate with the quote.

Do I need a permit to repipe in Union County?

A repipe is a change in plumbing and is normally permitted. Confirm with Union County Building Code Enforcement.

Will I be without water during a repipe?

Briefly. A good crew plans the swap to keep the gap short.

Can I repipe only part of the house?

Yes, when the rest of the system is sound.
