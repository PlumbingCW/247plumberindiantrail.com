Local Water

What Union County Water Reports About Your Tap

Most Indian Trail homes are served by Union County Water. According to its 2023 water quality report, the system listed average hardness of 23 ppm, which is very soft water, with an average pH of 7.11 and chloramine as the disinfectant. That means heavy scale is less common here than in hard-water towns, and most failures come from age, fittings and pressure.

While You Wait

What To Do Before The Plumber Arrives

Water is flowing

Close the main valve near the meter or where the line enters the house, then open the lowest faucet to drain the pipes.

Near electrical

Stay out of standing water near outlets and the panel. Shut off power at the breaker only if you can reach it dry.

Water heater leaking

Turn off the power or set the gas control to off, then close the cold valve on top of the tank.

Sewage backing up

Stop using every fixture and keep children and pets away from the room.

Gas smell

Leave the house, call the gas utility or 911 from outside, then call us.

Plumbing Across Indian Trail

Indian Trail grew from 1,942 residents in 1990 to 39,997 in the 2020 census, so most of the housing here is newer than in nearby county seats. Newer homes bring PVC drains and modern supply lines, but they also bring original water heaters now 15 or more years old and fittings that were rushed during building booms.

When we meet a call on Old Monroe Road, Unionville-Indian Trail Road or Potter Road, we ask the house age first because it shapes what we bring.
