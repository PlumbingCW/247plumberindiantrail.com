Bonterra Village is a master-planned community on the Old Monroe Road, Wesley Chapel Road and Sun Valley side of Indian Trail. Livability describes a mix of detached houses, townhomes and patio homes, and Walton Global's property page describes a 234-acre master plan with 626 single-family homes in its land use section. If something in your Bonterra Village home is leaking or backing up, call (704) 951-1635 and describe the problem.

If water is running, find the main shutoff and close it. In a townhome or patio home it may sit by the meter or in a utility closet, so locate it before you need it.

A neighborhood with an equestrian theme

Bonterra Village is known for its horse theme. Many streets are named after famous racehorses, including Seabiscuit Drive, Phar Lap Drive, Equipoise Drive and Belmont Stakes Avenue, and a 2025 Praise Charlotte report put the community at more than 800 residents. Those street names are a handy way to place an address on a call.

Our research found no published boundary map, so Bonterra Village Way and the street names above serve as anchors and no exact edges are claimed. The Town of Indian Trail's Northgate project lists 196 single-family lots at the corner of Bonterra Village Way and Northgate Boulevard, so newer homes are also being built nearby.

Why home type changes the plumbing questions

Livability notes that attached and patio properties may have different association obligations than detached houses. For plumbing, that difference is practical. A leak in one attached home can reach a neighbor's wall or ceiling, so the first call is often about stopping the water fast and documenting damage.

Detached homes

Most calls follow the usual pattern of leaks, drains, water heaters and fixtures, and the main shutoff is typically near the meter or where the line enters.

Townhomes

Know where your own shutoff is and which side of a shared wall a pipe runs through. Tell the neighbor quickly if water may be reaching their unit.

Patio homes

Smaller footprints put the water heater, laundry and bathrooms close together, so one leak can affect several rooms.

Insurance and HOA

Ask your HOA and insurer who is responsible for damage that crosses unit lines. We cannot answer that for you.

Before you call about a leak

Main shutoff

Close it, then run the lowest faucet to empty the lines.

Neighbor next door

In an attached home, knock and warn them if water may be crossing the wall.

Heater closet

Switch off power or gas and shut the cold valve above the tank.

Pictures

Photograph the damage and the leak source before cleanup starts.

Bonterra Village plumbing questions

I live in a Bonterra Village townhome. Who is responsible for a leak?

That depends on where the leak starts and what your HOA documents and insurance say. A pipe serving only your unit is usually yours. Ask your HOA and insurer about damage that reaches a neighbor.

Where is the main shutoff in a townhome or patio home?

It is often near the water meter or in a utility closet or garage. Find it now, turn it once so you know it works, and tell everyone in the house where it is.

Does the HOA handle plumbing inside my home?

Associations generally cover common areas. Interior plumbing is normally the owner's job, though attached homes can carry shared-wall rules, so read your covenants.

Is Bonterra Village inside Indian Trail?

Yes. Walton's property page places it in the town of Indian Trail, Union County, and the Town's Northgate project page lists the Bonterra Village Way intersection in Indian Trail.

My water heater is leaking in a patio home closet. What now?

Turn off power or gas, close the cold supply valve above the tank and call. See the water heater repair page for what the work involves.
