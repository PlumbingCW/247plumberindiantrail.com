Crismark is one of Indian Trail's largest established planned communities, in the northern part of town. Livability says its entrances connect to Idlewild Road, Mill Grove Road and Stevens Mill Road. If a drain, water heater or pipe in your Crismark home is giving you trouble, call (704) 951-1635 and describe what is happening.

When water will not stop, close the main valve at the meter or where the pipe enters the house first. Then call and ask whether plumbing service reaches your street.

About Crismark and The Enclaves

Crismark was built in phases. One real estate directory says homes were built between 2002 and 2019 by Eastwood Homes, and Livability describes the construction as primarily from the 2000s and 2010s. The Enclaves at Crismark covers later phases, with larger and more contemporary floor plans than many earlier streets.

Homes are mostly detached, with ranch, one-and-a-half-story and two-story layouts, and some larger ones have three-car garages. The clubhouse area has two pools and trails run past community ponds. Because we found no published boundary map, Crismark is described by its entrance roads and not by exact edges.

The local detail that matters: which utility serves you

Most of Indian Trail is served by Union County Water. The Town's own utilities page says Carolina Water Services of NC serves a small part of the Beacon Hills and Crismark neighborhoods. That is unusual for a single neighborhood, so it is worth checking before you call anyone about a meter, a main or a water quality question.

Union County Water

The Town lists Union County Water for most of Indian Trail. Its number on the Town's page is 704-296-4210.

Carolina Water Services of NC

The Town says it serves a small part of Crismark and Beacon Hills. Its number on the Town's page is 1-800-525-7990.

How to check

Your water bill names the provider. If you cannot find a bill, ask a neighbor on your street which company bills them.

In practice, the utility looks after the street main and usually the meter. The line from the meter into the house is generally the owner's. If you see water bubbling up at the curb, call your utility first. If the leak is on the house side, call a plumber.

What larger homes mean for plumbing

Listings in Crismark range from about 1,500 square feet to more than 5,000. A large home tends to add bathrooms, supply runs and demand on one hot water tank. When a heater in a larger home starts running out of hot water mid-shower, the cause can be sediment, a failed element or a unit that is simply sized small for the household.

None of that is a defect in the neighborhood. It is the same set of questions any larger suburban home raises, and a plumber can check the heater before you replace it.

Crismark plumbing questions

Is Crismark served by Union County Water?

Mostly, according to the Town of Indian Trail, which lists Union County Water for most of town. The Town also says a small part of Crismark is served by Carolina Water Services of NC. Your water bill tells you which applies to your house.

What do I do if my bill comes from Carolina Water Services?

Use the number on your bill for meter, main and billing questions. Plumbing from the meter to the house works the same way regardless of provider.

Does the Crismark HOA cover plumbing inside my home?

The association maintains the clubhouse, pools and trails. Pipes inside a house are normally the owner's job, and the covenants for your section say where the line is drawn.

My Enclaves home is large. Does that change my water heater choice?

It can. A bigger household draws more hot water, so capacity matters. The tankless water heater page explains when that option makes sense.

Which road is best for describing my address on a call?

Give your street name and the nearest entrance road, such as Idlewild Road, Mill Grove Road or Stevens Mill Road, and say what failed.
