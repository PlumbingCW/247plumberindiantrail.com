A water heater that quits at 6 a.m. or starts leaking at midnight is an emergency in any house with kids, a washer in daily use or a basement ceiling below the tank.

Our line is answered 24/7, and the plumber who picks up can tell you in a couple of minutes whether this is a shutoff-and-wait situation or something that needs a truck tonight. Water heater repair in Indian Trail, NC starts with that call, not with a booking form.

While you wait, do three things. Switch off the breaker for an electric unit or turn the gas control to pilot or off for a gas unit, then close the cold water inlet valve on top of the tank. If water is pooling, keep people and pets away from wet floors near the unit and any outlets.

When a water heater problem cannot wait

Some failures are annoying and some are urgent. Water running from the tank body or the bottom seam means the steel shell has failed, and no repair will stop it. Water at the temperature and pressure relief valve discharge pipe can mean excess pressure or a failing valve, so shut off the heat and the cold supply and call.

A gas smell near the unit is different. Leave the house, call the gas utility or 911 from outside, and call us after. Never relight a pilot you can smell gas around. The rest of the symptoms below can usually wait for a same-day appointment when one is available.

No hot water at all

On an electric unit this points to a tripped limit switch, a dead upper element or a failed thermostat. On gas it is usually the pilot, thermocouple or gas control valve.

Lukewarm water that runs out fast

A burned-out lower element or a sediment-choked tank is the usual cause. Hot water arrives, then fades after a few minutes of shower.

Popping or rumbling

Sediment baked onto the bottom of the tank traps steam bubbles. A flush often helps, and a heavily layered tank may be near the end of its life.

Rusty or metallic water

If only the hot side runs brown, the tank liner or anode rod is giving out. If both sides do, the problem is upstream of the heater.

Pilot will not stay lit

A worn thermocouple, a dirty pilot tube or a draft problem in the flue. Each has a different fix, so a guess wastes a visit.

Water around the base

Check the fittings and the relief valve first. A wet bottom with no fitting leak means the tank itself has failed.

How we find the fault

Diagnosis takes a multimeter, a gas pressure gauge for gas units and a careful look at the flue. On electric heaters we test the elements for resistance and the thermostats and high-limit for continuity, because a heating element that looks fine can still read open. On gas units we check the thermocouple output, the gas valve operation and the draft at the draft hood, since backdrafting is a safety issue and not just an efficiency one.

We also pull the anode rod when the tank is reachable. A rod worn down to the core wire tells us the tank has been under attack for a while and changes the repair-or-replace math. You get the readings, the part list and a clear price before any work starts.

What Indian Trail water does to a tank

Union County Water, which serves most of Indian Trail, reported average hardness of 23 ppm in its 2023 water quality report, about 1.3 grains per gallon. That is very soft water. Thick scale on elements is less common here than in hard-water towns, so a dead element is more often plain age or a failed thermostat than a limescale crust.

Permits and who should do the work

Union County Building Code Enforcement says on its residential page that replacing a water heater is exempt from a permit only if there is no change in fuel, energy source, location, capacity or the routing of venting and piping. Swapping a part inside the existing unit is plainly repair. Moving the heater, changing its fuel or resizing the vent can trigger a permit, so confirm with the county before work begins.

Plumbing contractors in North Carolina are licensed by the State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors. You can check any plumber's license on the board's public license search before you let anyone open a gas line or a water heater.

Codes and sources for water heater work

Water heaters in North Carolina are installed and repaired to the State Plumbing Code, which adopts the International Plumbing Code (IPC), together with the fuel gas code for gas units and the manufacturer's manual. Union County Building Code Enforcement lists the permit exemption for like-for-like replacement quoted above, and the NC licensing board keeps the public license search.

A leaking water heater: where the water comes from

A water heater leak does not always mean the tank is done. Check where the water originates before you decide.

Water at the inlet and outlet pipes at the top may be a loose connection or a failed flex connector. Water from the relief valve or its discharge pipe means pressure or temperature is too high or the valve is worn. Water at the drain valve near the bottom may be a loose valve. Water coming from the bottom of the tank body with no fitting wet is a failed tank.

Shut off the power or gas and the cold supply, and place a pan or towels under the unit. A small fitting leak is a repair. A leaking tank is a replacement. Take a photo of where the water is coming from, since it helps us tell you over the phone which case you have.

No hot water at night

No hot water is among the most common after-hours calls. For electric units, check the breaker and the red reset button on the upper thermostat. For gas units, check that the gas valve is on and see whether the pilot is lit by following the label on the tank. If it will not light after one careful try, stop and call. A gas unit that will not light, or a tank that trips the reset repeatedly, needs a plumber.

Water heater repair questions from Indian Trail

Do you handle water heater repair emergencies at night or on weekends in Indian Trail?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, including weekends and holidays. If the tank is leaking or you smell gas, we give you shutoff steps over the phone first, then send a plumber as soon as one is available.

Can you tell me over the phone if I need a new tank?

Often we can narrow it down. Water from the tank body or a rusted seam means replacement. A dead element, a bad thermostat or a pilot problem usually means repair. We confirm on site before quoting.

Is lukewarm water a repair or a replacement?

It depends on the age and what the diagnosis finds. A burned-out lower element is a modest repair on a younger tank. On an older tank with heavy sediment, the repair may only buy a little time.

Do you service tankless units too?
